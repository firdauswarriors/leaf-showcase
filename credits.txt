A greener Earth starts with a leaf.

Design: Leaf light botanical showcase, evolved from the user-supplied cinematic template. Apple HIG principles applied using the reviewed local dickwu/apple-design-skill (https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill). This project is not affiliated with Apple. Original shared plant-record SVG created for this website.
Botanical video: supplied by the user at https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/user_38xzZboKViGWJOttwIXH07lWA1P/hf_20260511_080827_a9e5ad52-b6ee-4e79-b393-d936f179cfd7.mp4
Delivery is a locally rendered 4.75-second forward/reverse growth loop, with 3328×2492 desktop and 1280×958 phone sources. Opening poster extracted from the delivered video’s first frame to avoid a stage change on loading. The artwork is illustrative and does not depict a measured Leaf pilot.
Team photographs: supplied by the team; existing approved crops retained.
Fonts: local system UI and serif stacks; no font files are embedded or downloaded.
